GTA6 Clipper

GTA6 Clipper is a private tool used by a single YouTube channel owner. It makes short clips of GTA 6 gameplay streams. Clips are uploaded to the owner's own YouTube channel as private. The owner reviews every clip, and only approved clips are made public and posted to the owner's own Facebook Page.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

GTA6 Clipper uses YouTube API Services. Use of the tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
