GTA6 Clipper – Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GTA6 Clipper is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel ("the owner"). It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

Use of YouTube API Services

GTA6 Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What information it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google OAuth, GTA6 Clipper accesses the owner's own YouTube channel to:

  • upload short videos to the channel (videos.insert);
  • change the title, description and visibility of videos it uploaded (videos.update);
  • list and, when the owner asks, delete those videos (videos.list, videos.delete);
  • read the channel's name and ID to confirm it is connected to the right channel (channels.list).

It does not access any other channel, and it does not access data about viewers, subscribers, comments or other YouTube users.

What it stores, and for how long

It stores only the IDs and titles of videos it uploaded to the owner's channel, so it can track what was posted. It also stores the OAuth refresh token needed to keep uploading, in a private, access-controlled environment. It does not store any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

Stored video IDs and titles are kept only while they are needed to manage those videos, and are deleted within 30 days after the owner revokes access or stops using the tool.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos. It is not sold, and it is not shared with any third party.

Cookies and device information

GTA6 Clipper does not place cookies on, or store or read information from, the devices of anyone who views the owner's videos.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, the tool can no longer reach the channel, and its stored data is deleted within 30 days. The owner can also ask for immediate deletion using the contact below.
